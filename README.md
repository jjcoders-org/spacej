
# SpaceJ

**Building aerospace technology, software, and infrastructure.**

SpaceJ is an early-stage aerospace initiative originating in India.

Our long-term ambition is to develop capabilities across launch systems, satellites, spacecraft, propulsion, orbital infrastructure, mission systems, and space exploration.

We are starting with research, software, simulation, and engineering. Our initial product will be selected through industry research and real user feedback.

**Build what is beyond.**

## Our mission

Develop useful aerospace technologies and progressively build the engineering capabilities and infrastructure required to participate across the space industry.

## Our vision

Become an integrated global aerospace company capable of designing, developing, and operating systems across Earth, orbit, and deep space.

## Our current stage

- Research and discovery.
- Aerospace software and engineering exploration.
- Identification of real industry problems.
- Development of our first technical project.

**Current capabilities:** We do not currently operate rockets, satellites, spacecraft, launch facilities, or flight-qualified systems.

Our first product has not yet been selected.

## Long-term technology areas

1. SpaceJ Launch — Launch vehicles and orbital transportation.
2. SpaceJ Orbital — Satellites and spacecraft.
3. SpaceJ Systems — Propulsion, avionics, navigation, and engineering systems.
4. SpaceJ Intelligence — Mission software, simulations, and space data.
5. SpaceJ Infrastructure — Orbital services and in-space logistics.
6. SpaceJ Exploration — Lunar and deep-space exploration.

These are long-term ambitions, not existing products.

## How we build

1. Identify a genuine problem.
2. Research existing solutions.
3. Speak with potential users and experts.
4. Define a technically achievable solution.
5. Build a working prototype.
6. Validate it with evidence.
7. Improve it based on real feedback.
8. Expand into more advanced aerospace technologies.
