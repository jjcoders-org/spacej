
# SpaceJ — Research Repository

## Research objective

Understand existing aerospace technologies, engineering systems, software, and industry needs before committing to our first product.

## Research Area 1 — Astrodynamics

Topics:
- Newtonian mechanics.
- Orbital mechanics.
- Keplerian orbital elements.
- Coordinate reference frames.
- Orbital perturbations.
- Orbit propagation.
- Mission trajectories.

## Research Area 2 — Spacecraft Engineering

Topics:
- Structures.
- Electrical power systems.
- Thermal management.
- Communication systems.
- Attitude determination and control.
- Onboard computing.
- Systems engineering.

## Research Area 3 — Aerospace Software

Investigate:

1. NASA GMAT
   https://github.com/NASA/GMAT

2. Basilisk
   https://avslab.github.io/basilisk/

3. CelesTrak
   https://celestrak.org/NORAD/documentation/gp-data-formats.php

Study their capabilities, limitations, licences, architecture, and potential applications.

Do not build a competing product before understanding what these tools already solve.

## Research Area 4 — Space Industry

Investigate:

- SpaceX.
- Rocket Lab.
- Blue Origin.
- Skyroot Aerospace.
- Agnikul Cosmos.
- Pixxel.
- Dhruva Space.
- Bellatrix Aerospace.

For each company document:

- Main products.
- Target customers.
- Technology.
- Revenue model.
- Competitive advantages.
- Known challenges.
- Opportunities for new entrants.
