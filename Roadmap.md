
# SpaceJ — Engineering Roadmap

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Foundational roadmap
**Date:** 17 November 2026

## North Star

Build an integrated aerospace company with capabilities spanning launch vehicles, spacecraft, propulsion, satellites, orbital infrastructure, and exploration.

The ambition is broad. Execution will be staged.

## Stage 0 — Foundation and discovery

Status: CURRENT

Objectives:

- Understand the aerospace industry.
- Study existing engineering tools.
- Identify problems faced by real aerospace teams.
- Research possible commercial opportunities.
- Build a technical knowledge foundation.
- Identify experienced potential mentors.

Exit criteria:

A specific engineering problem supported by real user evidence.

## Stage 1 — First engineering product

Potential areas to investigate:

- Astrodynamics software.
- Mission analysis.
- Satellite operations.
- Spacecraft simulation.
- Telemetry analysis.
- Ground-station software.

Objectives:

- Select one problem.
- Write a technical specification.
- Build a working prototype.
- Test against established references.
- Obtain feedback from knowledgeable users.

Exit criteria:

A functional, technically validated prototype with a clearly identified use case.

## Stage 2 — Commercial validation

Objectives:

- Identify potential customers.
- Test real demand.
- Develop a sustainable business model.
- Obtain initial revenue or suitable non-dilutive funding.
- Establish technical partnerships.

Exit criteria:

Evidence of repeatable demand and a sustainable product direction.

## Stage 3 — Physical space systems

Potential capabilities:

- Satellite-related hardware.
- Electronics and avionics prototypes.
- Payload systems.
- Ground infrastructure.
- Integration and testing.

Requires qualified engineering personnel, suitable facilities, safety procedures, and legal review.

Exit criteria:

A tested engineering subsystem with independent technical review.

## Stage 4 — Spacecraft and missions

Develop capabilities across:

- Spacecraft systems engineering.
- Satellite development.
- Payload integration.
- Ground operations.
- Mission planning.
- Authorised space missions.

Exit criteria:

A qualified team, verified systems, appropriate funding, partners, and necessary authorisations.

## Stage 5 — Launch systems

Long-term ambition:

- Launch vehicle development.
- Propulsion technologies.
- Structural engineering.
- Avionics and flight systems.
- Manufacturing and testing.
- Launch infrastructure.

This requires substantial funding, specialist facilities, experienced aerospace leadership, independent safety review, and regulatory approvals.

No physical rocket development or testing is authorised by this roadmap.

## Stage 6 — Integrated space infrastructure

Potential future capabilities:

- Reusable launch systems.
- Satellite networks.
- Orbital transportation.
- In-space servicing.
- Space infrastructure.
- Lunar exploration.
- Deep-space missions.

## Roadmap rules

Every stage must have measurable exit criteria.

We do not advance because a date arrives.

We advance when the engineering, business, safety, financial, and regulatory requirements are satisfied.

Review this roadmap monthly.
